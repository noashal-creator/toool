Perforated metal bird figure

The perforated metal bird figure is a small pressed-metal object, approximately 9 cm in height, pierced on the front by a circular perforated panel. The species depicted has not been identified.

Description

The body is hollow and ovoid. Eleven hemispherical studs are distributed across it. Two are where the eyes would be. Nine are not.

Function

Not documented.[1]
